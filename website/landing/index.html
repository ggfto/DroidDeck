Features

Everything a deck should do. On hardware you already own.

Buttons sized to your screen, with live state: they light up when you are muted, recording or the lamp is on.

Shortcuts & apps

Key combos, open programs, files and links, and multi-actions that chain steps with delays.

Audio mixer

Mute any app, control speakers and mic, plus a full per-app mixer on the phone.

Media

Play, pause and skip on Spotify, YouTube or any player Windows knows about.

Live monitors

CPU, GPU, RAM and network gauges, refreshed every second.

Discord

Mute, deafen, join channels, push-to-talk, per-user volume and the native soundboard.

OBS Studio

Scenes, recording, streaming, virtual camera and replay buffer, in sync with OBS.

Soundboard

Search the MyInstants library and play sounds straight into your mic for calls and streams.

Smart home

Tuya / Smart Life lights and plugs, with dimmers, colours and real state. No developer account needed.

Profiles & folders

Swipe between pages, nest folders, and edit everything with drag and drop in the browser.

How it works

Up and running in three minutes.

Run it on the PC

Unzip and open DroidDeck.exe. It sits quietly in the system tray. No installer, no .NET to install.

Pair the phone

Install the app, then scan the QR code from the tray menu. It reconnects by itself from then on.

Build your deck

Open localhost:4787 in the PC browser, drag buttons around and pick actions. Changes reach the phone instantly.

Integrations

Plays well with your setup.

Native integrations, no plugins to hunt down. Works with any Tuya-based brand, such as Nova Digital, Positivo and RSmart.

  • Local first. Phone and PC talk directly over your own network. No account to create.
  • Paired, not open. Every request needs the key from your QR code; the browser editor only works on the PC itself.
  • Light on the PC. Stats are only collected while a phone is connected.
  • Hackable. A documented REST + SignalR API lets you script it or build your own client.
# toggle Discord mute from anywhere
curl -X POST http://pc:4787/api/StreamDeck/execute \
  -H "X-API-KEY: $KEY" \
  -d '{"type":"discord","parameters":{"operation":"toggleMute"}}'
